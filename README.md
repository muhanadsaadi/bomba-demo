# Bomba Burrito – CONCEPT DEMO (Barza | برزة)

**Concept demo only. This is not an official Bomba Burrito menu.** It was built by Barza to show the restaurant what a bilingual QR menu with WhatsApp ordering could look like.

- Items and prices are transcribed from the restaurant's August 2026 PDF menu. They may contain errors.
- The WhatsApp number is a **dummy placeholder** (96800000000), so no real orders are sent.
- The menu engine is [barza-menu](https://github.com/muhanadsaadi/barza-menu); its CSS/JS are loaded from that site.
- Brand names and trademarks belong to their owners.

Live: https://muhanadsaadi.github.io/bomba-demo/
